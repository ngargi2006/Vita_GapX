About Vita-Gap

An open-source bioinformatics platform for assessing bacterial micronutrient autonomy and microbial metabolic cross-feeding.

Mission

B-vitamins and vitamin K2 play foundational catalytic roles across all kingdoms of life. Yet in complex microbial communities such as the human gut microbiome or soil ecosystems, many bacteria lack de novo synthesis pathways and rely on metabolic cross-feeding from neighboring species. Vita-Gap was engineered to distinguish true auxotrophs from organisms with unannotated phosphatase gaps, alternative biosynthetic routes, or fragmented draft assemblies.
