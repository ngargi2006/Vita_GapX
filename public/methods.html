Methodology & Computational Architecture

Vita-Gap evaluates bacterial vitamin biosynthesis potential using committed key-step weighting, taxonomic ambiguity resolution, and assembly-calibrated evidence scoring.

1. Biological Principles

  • Gene Name Ambiguity Across Phyla: Gene symbols like ribC refer to riboflavin synthase in E. coli but bifunctional riboflavin kinase/FAD synthetase in B. subtilis. Matching is anchored on KEGG Orthology (KO) IDs.
  • Alternative Pathways: Independent biosynthetic routes exist (e.g. DXP-dependent vs DXP-independent PLP; aerobic vs anaerobic B12; classical vs futalosine K2). Vita-Gap evaluates every route and reports the optimal variant.
  • Known Biological Gaps: Phosphatase steps (such as ArPP phosphatase in riboflavin or dihydroneopterin phosphatase in folate) are performed by non-specific or unannotated enzymes and are never penalized.
  • Usage Genes in Auxotrophs: Activation enzymes (e.g. RibF, CoaA-E, BirA) are retained even in complete auxotrophs and do not constitute evidence for de novo synthesis.

2. Completeness Metrics

For each pathway variant, steps are weighted according to their committed role:

  • Key Committed Steps (K): Weight = 2.0
  • Normal Pathway Steps (N): Weight = 1.0
  • Low Specificity Steps (L): Weight = 0.5
  • Known Biological Gaps (G) & Activation (A): Excluded from completeness denominator.
C_w = Σ (w_i · p_i) / Σ w_i
C_u = (Present Steps) / (Total Evaluated Steps)
KC = (Present Key Steps) / (Total Key Steps)

3. Calibrated Evidence Scoring (0–100)

The evidence score answers Q5 (how reliable is the prediction?) and prevents false auxotrophy calls from partial gene lists or draft MAGs:

E_found = Weighted mean of step match qualities (q) over detected steps
a (Absence Reliability) = Complete: 1.0 · Draft: 0.9 · MAG: CheckM / 100 · Open-world: 0.0
a_path = 1 - (1 - a)^k (k = independent genomic loci for the pathway)
Evidence Tiers: Strong (≥ 75), Moderate (50–74), Weak (< 50).
